# Client setup and portal access

## Find a company and see what it needs

As a lead, I can find a company in Clients and see whether its support portal is ready. I can search by company name and filter by portal status.

- WiseWork companies appear even before they have a Right Tracker workspace.
- Right Tracker-only companies appear in the same list.
- An existing workspace with an unavailable WiseWork link is marked for review. Its people and tickets remain accessible to administrators.

## Set up a portal

As an authorized lead, I can set up a WiseWork client's portal without retyping its company name. Right Tracker links the workspace to that exact WiseWork record and opens the company page. Repeated requests return the same workspace. If a standalone workspace already has the same name, the lead can review the existing record before linking it.

As an authorized lead, I can also create a standalone workspace for a company without WiseWork.

## Invite people and share sign-in

As an authorized lead, I can invite one or more people from the company page. Invitations default to access to the recipient's own requests. Company-wide ticket access is an explicit choice. Each recipient sets their own password from a personal activation link. The company page provides the shared client sign-in link for future visits.

## Manage existing access

As an authorized lead, I can inspect members and invitation status, revoke pending invitations, or issue a new invitation when one expires. The company page provides a filtered ticket list. WiseWork link repair is an admin setting.

## Pilot acceptance

- A technical lead and a non-technical lead can complete setup, invite a member and a manager, and copy the sign-in link without guidance.
- A member sees their own requests, a manager sees requests in their company, and a user from an unrelated company cannot see those requests.
- Existing memberships, tickets, and direct Organizations URLs remain usable during the transition.
- Invitation delivery and the complete customer activation flow are verified in the deployment environment before external rollout.
